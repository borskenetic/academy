> Curriculum provided for the Skillsoft learning catalog. Durations are shown in hours.

## Core Skillsoft Courses

### 1. AI for Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

**Duration:** 30 hours

**Course description:** Introduces the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Data Analytics and Business Intelligence (BI). Learners explore how AI can collect, prepare, analyze, visualize, and interpret data; generate insights; automate reporting; and support data-driven business decisions. The course combines analytics concepts with Generative AI, predictive analytics, dashboards, and intelligent BI tools.

**Learning outcomes:**

- Understand AI-driven data analytics and BI and their role in business decision-making.
- Prepare and clean datasets using AI-assisted techniques.
- Apply AI and Generative AI to structured and unstructured business data.
- Identify patterns, trends, correlations, and anomalies.
- Create dashboards and visualizations that communicate insights.
- Use AI-assisted predictive analytics for forecasting and planning.
- Automate routine reporting and data analysis.
- Develop prompts for extracting insights and generating analytical outputs.
- Translate data insights into recommendations for marketing, finance, HR, operations, and sales.
- Apply responsible AI and data governance principles, including privacy, security, bias, and ethics.
