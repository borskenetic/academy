# Skillsoft Partner Page Phase 1: Audit and Content Refinement

## Goal

Prepare the Skillsoft partner page for redesign and implementation by clarifying the audience, primary action, content hierarchy, and copy direction.

## Primary Page Objective

The page should convert eligible visitors into active Skillsoft learners by helping them understand the partnership, trust the offer, and take the next step toward course access.

Primary action:

- Explore Skillsoft Courses

Secondary actions:

- View Certifications
- Learn About Access
- Student Login
- Register

## Audience Priorities

The page should speak first to learners who want career-relevant skills and certifications, then to faculty and institutions evaluating academic value.

Primary audiences:

- Students who want employable skills, certifications, and guided learning paths.
- Faculty who want current learning resources for teaching and upskilling.
- Professionals who want flexible career development.
- Institutions that want stronger learning outcomes and employability support.

## Current Design Audit

The existing design has a strong foundation. It uses recognizable Skillsoft branding, a credible institutional tone, and a complete landing-page structure. The main issue is that the page asks users to absorb too much before giving them a clear primary action.

Keep:

- JNTUH Education Foundation identity in the header.
- Large Skillsoft presence in the hero.
- Navy and teal color direction.
- Learning-area card grid.
- Audience section.
- Impact statistics.
- Final call-to-action band.
- Multi-column footer.

Improve:

- Add a primary CTA directly in the hero.
- Shorten dense body copy.
- Reduce repeated claims across the trust strip and benefit sections.
- Make the strongest proof points easier to find.
- Improve readability for small copy.
- Plan stronger mobile stacking for card-heavy sections.

## Proposed Page Hierarchy

1. Header
2. Hero with immediate CTA
3. Trust markers
4. About Skillsoft and access benefits
5. Learning areas
6. Audience fit
7. Impact statistics
8. Final CTA
9. Footer

## Content Questions to Resolve Before Final Build

- What exact users are eligible for Skillsoft access?
- Does "Register" create a JNTUH Education Foundation account, a Skillsoft account, or both?
- Should "Student Login" and "Register" remain separate flows?
- Are the impact statistics in the screenshot final and verified?
- What destination URLs should each CTA and learning-area card use?
- Are there required legal, partner, or brand guidelines for Skillsoft logo usage?

## Implementation Notes

- Use the same primary CTA label in the hero and final CTA unless product requirements dictate otherwise.
- Keep body copy concise and avoid repeating the same benefit in multiple sections.
- Use real verified numbers for impact statistics, or remove the section until they are confirmed.
- Ensure every card with a hover state is also keyboard focusable if it behaves as a link.
- Add analytics events to all primary and secondary actions.

## Phase 1 Completion Criteria

- Primary and secondary page actions are defined.
- Audience order is defined.
- Draft copy is available for each major section.
- Open content questions are documented.
- The next phase can begin with wireframe revision.
